**Breaking:** features move into plugin packages listed once in `ogygia({ plugins: [...] })`: flags (`@ogygia/flags`, was `ogygia/flag`), frozen pages (`@ogygia/freeze`, was `ogygia/freeze` and `ogygia({ freeze })`), fragment federation (`@ogygia/federation`, was `ogygia/federation`; `mount` moved from `ogygia/router`) and content (`@ogygia/content`, was `ogygia/content*` and `ogygia({ content })`; `ogygia/content/server` is `@ogygia/content/remote`, and the svelte config's `ogygia.preprocess()` / `ogygia.extensions()` are `preprocess()` / `extensions()` from `@ogygia/content/vite`), each with its factory from `<package>/vite`. The devtools dock and the profiler move to `@ogygia/devtools`: `ogygia({ devtools, profiler })` becomes `plugins: [devtools({ dock, profiler })]`, `ogygia/profiler*` imports become `@ogygia/devtools/profiler*` (`@ogygia/devtools/profiler`, `/profiler/client`, `/profiler/storage`, `/profiler/storage/sqlite|redis|postgres`), and `ogygia/devtools` is now the event layer alone (as `ogygia/events`). `@ogygia/tsdown-plugin-svelte` turns a component's relative `./x.ts` imports into `./x.js` in the shipped component and its types, so sources can import with real extensions.
